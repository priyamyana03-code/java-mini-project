Recommended Tools

Recommended Art Supplies

Equip yourself with the right materials handpicked by our mentors for each artistic discipline.

For Sketching
  • HB Pencil (Light outlines)
  • 2B Pencil (Mid-shading)
  • 4B Pencil (Deep shadows)
  • Sketchbook (140+ GSM)
  • Kneaded / Mono Eraser
For Painting
  • Stretched Canvas / Board
  • Acrylic Paints (Primary set)
  • Flat & Round Brushes
  • Mixing Palette
  • Water Cup & Rag Cloth
For Pastel
  • Soft Pastels (24/36 set)
  • Textured Pastel Paper
  • Blending Stump (Tortillon)
  • Art Fixative Spray
  • Wet Wipes for fingers
For Doodling
  • Black Fineliner (0.3 & 0.5)
  • Plain White Sketchbook
  • Gel Pens (Colored set)
  • Ruler / Compass (for mandalas)
  • Dual Tip Brush Markers
